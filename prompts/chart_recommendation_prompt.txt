# CHART RECOMMENDATION STRATEGY

Based on the shape, columns, and data types of query results, recommend the best visualization chart format.

## Mapping Table
1. **Time Series (Date/Time on X-axis, Numeric on Y-axis)**:
   - *Recommendation*: **Line Chart**
   - *Reason*: Visualizes continuous trends over date intervals.
2. **Categorical Comparisons (String on X-axis, Numeric on Y-axis)**:
   - *Recommendation*: **Bar Chart** (Horizontal or Vertical)
   - *Reason*: Highlights differences between distinct categories or entities.
3. **Parts-to-Whole Composition (Sum of values is 100% or represents distribution)**:
   - *Recommendation*: **Pie Chart** or **Donut Chart**
   - *Reason*: Displays relative proportions of segments.
4. **Correlations & Distributions (Numeric on X-axis, Numeric on Y-axis)**:
   - *Recommendation*: **Scatter Plot**
   - *Reason*: Reveals correlation patterns between metrics.
